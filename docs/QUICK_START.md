# 📸 Profile Upload - Quick Start Guide

## 🎯 How to Upload Your Photo

### Step 1: Navigate to Home
Open your portfolio and go to the **Home** page

### Step 2: Hover Over Profile
Move your mouse over the **profile image** on the right side

### Step 3: Click Upload Button
Click the **"Upload Photo"** button that appears

### Step 4: Select Image
Choose your professional photo:
- ✅ JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP format
- ✅ Maximum 5MB file size
- ✅ Recommended: 800x1000px (4:5 ratio)

### Step 5: Wait for Success
Watch the loading animation and success confirmation

### Step 6: Done! 🎉
Your photo is now **permanently saved** and will never be removed!

---

## 🔄 How to Change Your Photo

1. Hover over your current photo
2. Click **"Change Photo"** button
3. Select a new image
4. Done!

---

## 🗑️ How to Remove Custom Photo

**Option 1: Browser DevTools**
1. Press `F12` to open DevTools
2. Go to **Application** tab
3. Click **Local Storage** → Your domain
4. Find and delete `portfolio_profile_image`
5. Refresh the page

**Option 2: Clear Browser Data**
- Clear your browser's local storage
- This will also remove other saved data

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### "Please select a valid image file"
➡️ You selected a non-image file. Choose JPG, PNG, GIF, or WEBP

### "Image size should be less than 5MB"
➡️ Your image is too large. Compress it or choose a smaller file

### Upload button doesn't appear
➡️ Make sure you're **hovering** over the profile image

### Photo doesn't persist after refresh
➡️ Check if localStorage is enabled in your browser settings

## 💡 Tips for Best Results

1. **Use a professional photo**
   - Good lighting
   - Clear facial features
   - Professional attire

2. **Optimal dimensions**
   - 800x1000px (4:5 aspect ratio)
   - Or any size with 4:5 ratio

3. **File size**
   - Keep under 2MB for best performance
   - Compress if needed

4. **Background**
   - Solid color or professional setting
   - Avoid busy backgrounds
